# Standard Operating Procedure: Community Governance, Moderation & Escalation

**Author:** Arafat Alalade  
**Scope:** Multi-team moderation operations, risk escalation, and contributor onboarding across high-volume digital communities

## Purpose
Establish clear, repeatable operational standards for maintaining signal quality, user safety, and consistent rule enforcement across large community channels without relying on ad-hoc moderator judgment.

## Severity & Escalation Matrix

| Severity Level | Examples | Target Response | Action & Ownership |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SEV-1 (Critical)** | Compromised accounts, malicious links/drains, coordinated brigading, severe policy abuse | Immediate (< 5 min) | Lock affected channels, revoke permissions/ban offending accounts, escalate to senior leads |
| **SEV-2 (High)** | Targeted harassment, repeated rule evasion, impersonation, high-volume spam waves | Rapid (< 15 min) | Remove content, apply temporary timeout/mute or role restriction, log incident in mod tracker |
| **SEV-3 (Medium)** | Off-topic promotion, repetitive low-effort posts, mild friction or misunderstandings | Standard (< 1 hr) | Issue clear rule reminder, redirect thread to appropriate channel, warn if recurring |
| **SEV-4 (Low)** | Minor formatting/channel misrouting, general onboarding or navigation questions | Routine (< 4 hrs) | Guide user politely, pin/reference FAQ or channel rules |

## Moderator Onboarding & Calibration
1. **Rulebook & Edge-Case Walkthrough:** New moderators review community rules, tone standards, and real historical edge cases before receiving elevated permissions.
2. **Shadowing & Co-Moderation:** Pair incoming moderators with an experienced team member to observe enforcement decisions and rationale.
3. **Consistency Audits:** Hold periodic team check-ins to align on ambiguous cases, prevent over- or under-enforcement, and refine documentation.

## Documentation & Handoff Standards
- Record non-routine enforcement actions and unresolved escalations with timestamps, affected handles, evidence, and resolution status.
- Deliver concise shift handoff notes covering active incidents, watchlists, and pending policy questions so coverage stays seamless across time zones.
