# Technical & Protocol Research Portfolio

Long-form architectural analyses, whitepaper teardowns, generative systems research, and operational standard operating procedures (SOPs) authored by **Arafat Alalade**.

## Research Focus Areas
- **Protocol & Consensus Architecture:** Cryptographic settlement models, incentive design, and network security assumptions.
- **Generative Systems & Digital Artifacts:** Deterministic algorithms, on-chain data persistence, and emergent creative systems.
- **Operations & Community Governance:** Moderation frameworks, contributor onboarding SOPs, and risk escalation workflows.
